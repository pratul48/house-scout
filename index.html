🧭 Check it with my phone's compass

Stand at the front door with your back to the door, facing the street. Hold the phone flat with its top pointing straight out from the house, then tap Lock reading.

Phone compasses point to magnetic north, which in the US can be up to ~15° off true north. Look up your value at NOAA (e.g. Seattle ≈ +15, Dallas ≈ +3, New York ≈ −13).
